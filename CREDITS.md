## Thunder sound sources

The procedural thunder generator (`src/hooks/use-thunder.ts`) recombines short excerpts cut from the following [Freesound.org](https://freesound.org) recordings. Each source was denoised, sliced into short "crack" and "tail" excerpts, and peak-normalized before inclusion — none are used unmodified, which each license below permits.
